Service Areas

Dental Lab Services Across Malaysia's Key Regions

Curative supports dental clinics throughout Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, and extends the same digital scan-to-delivery workflow to clinics in Melaka, Seremban, Ipoh and Kuantan — digital case submission means clinic location is not a barrier. Singapore coverage is expanding soon.

Kuala Lumpur & Selangor Clinics

Serving clinics across Kuala Lumpur, Bangsar, Mont Kiara, Damansara, Petaling Jaya, Subang Jaya, Shah Alam, Puchong, Cheras and Klang.

Melaka Clinics

Melaka Tengah, Alor Gajah and Jasin clinics submit cases digitally via MeditLink or 3Shape Trios Communicate for zirconia, glass ceramic, implant restorations and surgical guides.

Seremban Clinics

Negeri Sembilan clinics gain the same digital workflow available to KL and Selangor practices — full crown, bridge, veneer and implant restoration range available via digital case submission.

Ipoh Clinics

Perak's largest concentration of private dental practices, served through Curative's digital workflow for zirconia and glass ceramic restorations, custom titanium abutments and digital smile design cases.

Kuantan Clinics

East coast Pahang clinics gain the most from a fully digital pipeline — implant surgical guides, restorations and retention appliances handled without physical impression transit.

Remote & Singapore Clinics

Clinics outside our named service areas, including Singapore, can also submit digital cases remotely. Contact the Curative team for current delivery arrangements.
